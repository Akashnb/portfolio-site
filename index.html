// services

Where I turn technology into product value.

AI can generate the code. What a senior engineer still brings is the judgment — the right architecture, a careful read of what the model got wrong, and a translation of business needs into something that actually ships.

Architecture & Technical Decisions

I choose the approach before code gets written — state, data flow, and structure that hold up as the product grows, not just the first release.

AI Output Verification & Code Review

I review what AI generates before it ships — catching security gaps, bad patterns, and edge cases the model missed, so shipping fast doesn't mean shipping risk.

Production Debugging & Incident Response

I diagnose live failures with incomplete logs and no clear repro steps, and get products back to stable without guesswork.

Requirements Translation

I turn a vague ask from product or a client into a spec an engineer — or an AI — can actually build, so less gets lost between the idea and the build.
